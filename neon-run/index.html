NEON RUN: The Crystal Hummingbird

In a world where light is power, darkness is taking over.

Long ago, the city of Neon was powered by the Crystal Heart, a mysterious source of energy hidden deep beneath the streets. But when the Crystal Hummingbird—a powerful guardian—became corrupted by a dark force, it shattered the Crystal Heart and stole its fragments.

Now the city is falling apart. Neon lights are fading, portals are opening, and dangerous creatures are spreading across the world.

You are the last runner capable of restoring the Crystal Heart. To save Neon, you must race through dangerous levels, defeat corrupted enemies, and collect the lost crystal fragments.

But at the end of your journey stands the Crystal Hummingbird.

Once a protector, now a deadly enemy, it guards the final piece of the Crystal Heart. You must defeat it—not to destroy the guardian, but to free it from the darkness.

Run fast. Fight hard. Restore the light.

HOW TO PLAY

  • MOVEA / D or ← → keys, gamepad stick / D-pad, or the on-screen arrows.
  • JUMPW, ↑ or SPACE, gamepad A / ✕, or the JUMP button on touch screens.
  • COLLECTGrab the glowing energy orbs for points and a bonus at each portal.
  • DEFEATStomp sentry bots from above. Avoid the neon spikes and don't fall.
  • PORTALReach the portal at the end of each of the three levels.
  • BOSSDodge the Hummingbird's shards and dive. When it crashes, stomp it. Five hits wins!
