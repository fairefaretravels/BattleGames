How to play

1. Players take turns rolling.
2. Odd rolls (1, 3, 5) are RED: go back 3 spaces.
3. Even rolls (2, 4, 6) are GREEN: move forward the number rolled.
4. Tap a glowing destination space to move. You must land exactly on HOME.
5. Purple spaces mean pull a chance card. Follow its instruction.
6. First player to reach HOME wins!
